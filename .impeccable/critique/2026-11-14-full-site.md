# Full-site review, 2026-11-14

Theo's verdict on the build: "way way below what I expect", "a bad hacked together Squarespace", "AI slop, not a highly bespoke artistic brewery website". He likes Home's first screen (one ink line: ground, hills, glass; the tap; the pour). Agreed plan: one system from that line; whole pages, not patches; independent review and a fix pass before he sees each page; preview links on `redesign/one-line`, nothing to main until he approves.

Captures: `.impeccable/review/site-2026-11-14/<page>/{p,d}-NN.png` (local only; taken from a build with the clock frozen on 2026-11-13, so "Fri 13 Nov" under Coming up and tonight's hours row are artefacts, not bugs). Three independent reviewers.

## The system (decisions)

- Cream paper on every page. No dark "overtime" pages, no green slab. One accent: the yellow pill button.
- The ink line (2 px, round caps, one hand) is the only ornament and each page's spine. Home: ground, hills, glass. Where it comes from: a delivery route. Visit: the street. Events: a timeline. Footer: the ground line again, ending at the seal.
- Fat serif for page titles and section heads; condensed bold for item names and labels; Barlow for running text.
- Section heads on Home, Visit and Events are a serif title on the line, not a black pill. Pills stay on the Menu only, where they are the printed menu's own device.
- Rows, not cards: hairlines between rows, no backgrounds, no radii, no stubs, no chips.
- One 12-column grid, the existing gutters (22 px phone, 88 px desktop), one section gap.
- Links: one style (ink underline). Buttons: yellow primary, ink secondary. Nothing else.

## Home (reviewer 1)

Why it reads templated: the line dies after the first screen; five visual languages below it; every heading the same black pill among ten other rounded shapes; "Where it comes from" is a table plus a stock state outline on the one colour Theo wants less of.

Defects: desktop On tap columns start at different heights and rules do not align; "5 / 8" unexplained on every row; kitchen photos soft and mismatched, a centred boxed paragraph over a left button; three of three coming events are Knicks; map labels collide; address headline heavier than the section heads; chips wrap raggedly; Gift cards a bare heading here and a pill on Visit; a 250 px void before the footer; three link styles.

Build:

1. First screen: keep. Refine the pour and how the ground line meets the foot (Theo).
2. On tap: line head with the "9 oz / 16 oz" legend; two columns whose group heads align.
3. From the kitchen: one photo in the grid, the burger line as plain left-aligned text, one link.
4. Coming up: rows (serif day numeral, condensed weekday and month, title, time), hairlines, one link.
5. Where it comes from, "the delivery run": the line as a route with a stop per town in site.json (Hudson Falls beef, Cairo buns, Altamont cider, Hammondsport wine) ending at Catskill where it becomes a glass; each stop a small drawing in the same stroke with what / who / where; grain and spirits (no town) as a caption; sourcingLine the only sentence. Phone: line down the left, stops to its right. Desktop: line across, stops under it. Interaction: the line draws with scroll; tapping a stop inks its drawing. Static with reduced motion.
6. Visit band: hours and address on the grid, no chips; gift cards one line.

## Visit (reviewer 1)

Why: it is Home's Visit band re-pasted; unrelated stacked blocks; generic UI icons and a one-off blue badge. Defects: address floats unaligned on desktop; two amateur phone shots in bordered cards beside a pro photo; a full-bleed photo that breaks the grid; Contact repeats the footer.

Build, "the street": one line draws 96 West Bridge Street in elevation (street, the garage door, the heated tent, 8 parking bays, the Division Street corner, a dog on a leash), labelled with the verbatim visit strings. Phone order: title and status; address with Directions and Call; the drawing; hours; one photo (fire pits); gift cards. Desktop: drawing across the grid, hours on columns 1 to 7, address and actions 8 to 12. Interaction: tap Parking, Tent or Dogs and that part inks yellow with its sentence beneath. Drop Contact.

## Events, event pages, header, footer, stubs, 404 (reviewer 3)

Why: Events is a different website (dark skin, photo hero, ticker, orange tickets, starburst, scoreboard); everything is a box or a pill; empty stubs under a footer taller than the page.

Defects: "Tonight" burst clips the title; "FINAL" under a game badged Tonight; the scoreboard is a sports-app widget in a blue the brand does not have; the ticker repeats the list; an orphan card in the month grid; the eyebrow repeats the title; a tilted torn ticket stub on event pages; a full-bleed blue band holding a small seal; three button styles in one cluster; the 404 glass drawn in a different hand; the Order pill is yellow on cream and salmon on dark; the header rule is solid in places and dashed in others; the active-page wedge crowds its neighbour; the footer is 720 px on a phone with six unranked items.

Build:

- Header: Order yellow on every page; one solid hairline on scroll; active page marked by a short ink stroke under it.
- Events list: cream, no photo, no ticker, no filters; the line down the left as a timeline with a knot at each date; rows (serif numeral, condensed day and month, title, time); tonight's row gets the glass. Desktop: one column to 720 px, tonight's event beside it.
- Event page: back link; date and time line; serif title on the line; description; good to know as plain lines; one primary action; Directions and Share as links; previous and next as text rows. No stub, scoreboard, band or countdown colour.
- Footer: cream; the ground line across, ending at the seal; status as text; one row of links on desktop, two columns on a phone; wordmark at header size; about 320 px on a phone.
- Stubs: Our beer and About out of the nav and footer until Max's content arrives; if reached, the title, the line trailing into an empty glass, links to Menu and Visit. Needs a TODO(Max) for any words.
- 404: redraw its glass in Home's hand, on the line.

## Menu, drinks and food (reviewer 2)

Why: everything is a pill or a box (eight rounded shapes in four styles on the first phone screen); three layouts for one kind of thing (burger cards on desktop but rows on a phone, sauces a third component, beer rows with an icon gutter); hierarchy inverted (item names as heavy as section heads, subgroup labels tiny), and the small glasses and the orange diamond rail are two more drawing languages.

Defects: sticky chrome takes about 23% of a phone screen (two control rows plus the bottom Order bar); Food's first phone screen shows no food and no price; descriptions truncated with an ellipsis and a small "+" that hides ingredients; two left edges (beer names at 60, everything else at 22); "5 / 8" explained once; three rule styles; the chip row clips mid-word; filters are six chips on desktop and hidden on a phone; the bottom Order bar duplicates the header; a section title wraps into a two-line black blob; centred text in boxes; desktop two-column orphans; yellow tags on drinks and beige on food; voids of about 250 px before the footer.

Build:

- One row everywhere: name left, price right in a fixed tabular column on the same baseline, the full description beneath, never truncated, no "+". Beer has two price columns headed "9 oz / 16 oz", repeated per subgroup. Burgers and sauces use the same row at every size.
- Section heads in the fat serif, plain ink, over one solid rule that carries the section's note at right. Subgroups in 13 px capitals. Item names in a lighter condensed weight, about 20 px. Solid hairlines only. Tags as plain small capitals. Intros as left-aligned paragraphs, no border.
- Phone: a one-line title block; one sticky bar of 48 px or less (Drinks | Food as text tabs, the current section as a button opening a full-sheet index with the filters, Order as a link). No chip row, no Filter button, no bottom bar. The first item above the fold on both tabs.
- Desktop: columns 1 to 3 a sticky text index with the filters beneath; columns 4 to 12 rows in two columns filled downward, no split items.
- The ink line, three uses only: the section rule lifts once per section into one small drawn object; the active tab or index marker is a short stroke; nothing else. Delete the per-beer glasses and the orange rail.
- Keep: one page with a Drinks/Food switch and deep links, right-aligned prices, all existing copy, the allergen wording.

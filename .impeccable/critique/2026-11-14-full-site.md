## The system (decisions)

- Cream paper on every page. No dark "overtime" pages, no green slab. One accent: the yellow pill button.
- The ink line (2 px, round caps, one hand) is the only ornament and each page's spine. Home: ground, hills, glass. Where it comes from: a delivery route. Visit: the street. Events: a timeline. Footer: the ground line again, ending at the seal.
- Fat serif for page titles and section heads; condensed bold for item names and labels; Barlow for running text.
- Section heads on Home, Visit and Events are a serif title on the line, not a black pill. Pills stay on the Menu only, where they are the printed menu's own device.
- Rows, not cards: hairlines between rows, no backgrounds, no radii, no stubs, no chips.
- One 12-column grid, the existing gutters (22 px phone, 88 px desktop), one section gap.
- Links: one style (ink underline). Buttons: yellow primary, ink secondary. Nothing else.

## Phone solidity pass, 2026-11-15 (from Theo's iPhone)

Reported: sheets cut off at the bottom; page content visible in the strip by the Dynamic
Island and behind Safari's bottom bar; links should land at the right scroll position.

What was found (developer write-ups on iOS 26 Safari, not Apple documentation): Safari
paints those two strips from the background colour of a fixed or sticky element touching
that edge, sampled a few pixels inside it, and shows the page's own pixels when there is
none; `theme-color` is ignored; the safe-area insets read 0 inside the browser; the
floating bar covers the lowest part of fixed elements.

Changed:

- The header no longer slides away on scroll on phones. It is the element Safari samples
  at the top.
- A 6 px fixed strip in the page's colour at the bottom edge (`.edge-bottom`), phones and
  tablets only; the root has an opaque background colour.
- Sheets: a column no taller than 86% of the small viewport, with a scrolling body that
  ends 88 px clear of the bottom.
- Links to a place on a page land below the pinned header (`scroll-padding-top`).
- Tap targets: footer links, the logo, the Food tab and the hero arrow are 44 px; text
  links get a larger target on touch screens without changing size.
- New `tests/e2e/phone.spec.ts`: header pinned, bottom strip, sheets fit, links land at
  the top, hash links land under the header, Back restores the position.
- A sweep of 11 pages at 320, 375, 393 and 430 px in WebKit: no sideways overflow, nothing
  past the screen edge, no text under 12 px, no tap target under 44 px after the fixes.

Not verified: the tinting itself. No test browser reproduces iOS 26 Safari's bars; this
needs Theo's phone. If content still shows at the bottom, the next thing to try is a
taller strip.
